06 / Questions

FAQ

Everything you need to know about ProLift Sports. Still have questions? Reach out to us directly.

What does ProLift Sports do?

ProLift Sports is an athlete management and sports media agency based in Nagpur, India. We help athletes build personal brands, create premium cinematic content, manage social media, negotiate brand collaborations, and produce commercial shoots. We represent premier cricketers including Team India and IPL players.

Where is ProLift Sports based?

ProLift Sports is based in Nagpur, Maharashtra, India. We work with athletes across India and have an international project called Vyom Sports in England, United Kingdom, expanding our reach into Europe.

Who are the athletes managed by ProLift Sports?

Our roster includes Yash Thakur (Team India / Lucknow Super Giants), Atharva Taide (PBKS / SRH), Darshan Nalkande (Gujarat Titans), and Apoorv Wankhade (Vidarbha, former Mumbai Indians / KKR). We also work with trusted athletes like Aman Mokhade (Irani Cup Winner), Shrikant Wagh, Disha Kasat (WPL Winner), and Saurabh Dubey.

What content packages does ProLift offer?

We offer three content packages: Single Reel (starter evaluation), 4 Reels Package (monthly momentum with consistent weekly touchpoints), and 8 Reels Package (full-scale 360° management for elite athletes). All packages include cinematic editing, sound design, and color grading. Contact us at prolift.pvt@gmail.com for package details and custom quotes.

What is the ProLift No Deal No Fee model?

For brand deals and collaborations, ProLift operates on a No Deal, No Fee basis. There are no joining fees. We pitch you to relevant brands, handle full negotiation and communication, and manage campaign coordination. We only earn when you earn. Our success is directly linked to yours.

How can brands partner with ProLift athletes?

Brands can partner with ProLift athletes through brand ambassadorships (long-term face of brand, TVCs), match and league campaigns (IPL-season activations), digital social content (high-engagement reels, product trials), and events and appearances (store launches, corporate keynotes, masterclasses). Contact us at prolift.pvt@gmail.com for partnership opportunities.

Does ProLift Sports work with athletes outside cricket?

While our primary roster is cricket-focused, ProLift Sports is equipped to work with athletes across sports including fitness, lifestyle, and youth culture. Our media production, branding, and social media management services apply to any sporting talent looking to build a professional brand.

What is the Vyom Sports international project?

Vyom Sports is ProLift Sports' international project based in England, United Kingdom. It is a cross-border sports media bridge expanding our reach into England and Europe, facilitating bilateral sports media and global brand campaigns for dual-market visibility.

How do I join ProLift as an athlete?

To join ProLift Sports, fill out the application form on our Join Us page, or contact us directly at prolift.pvt@gmail.com. You can also reach our founders: Arjun Gawre at +91 90282 41131 or Mayuresh Jumle at +91 83788 17364.

What makes ProLift different from other sports agencies?

ProLift offers turnkey in-house production with zero agency friction. We handle scripting, athlete scheduling, shoots, and editing in-house. Our content is engineered for algorithmic performance with 3-second hooks, kinetic subtitles, and broadcast-grade sound and color. We guarantee deliverables with clear contractual terms, category exclusivity, and disciplined delivery timelines.
